# Profile icon sources

The GitHub, LinkedIn and ResearchGate hero glyphs are embedded in
`src/components/shared/ProfileServiceIcon.astro`, sourced through the Supericons
connector on 2026-10-27. They inherit the link's currentColor and remain
decorative beside their visible service labels.

- GitHub: Tabler `brand-github` (MIT), https://tabler.io/icons/icon/brand-github
- LinkedIn: Tabler `brand-linkedin` (MIT), https://tabler.io/icons/icon/brand-linkedin
- ResearchGate: Simple Icons `researchgate` (CC0), https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons

The footer profile list and its markup are unchanged.
