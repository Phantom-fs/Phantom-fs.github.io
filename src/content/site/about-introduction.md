I am an AI researcher working across trustworthy clinical language systems, multimodal representation learning, synthetic-media forensics, agricultural intelligence, and scientific machine learning. As a Research Assistant at Nanyang Technological University, I develop and evaluate privacy-preserving clinical LLM workflows; through AI4A Lab, I connect research questions to public datasets, codebases, and applications.

My experience spans research roles at NTU, AI4A Lab, Ulster University, Manipal University Jaipur, NIT Jalandhar, and ULPGC. I value careful evaluation, reproducible artifacts, and collaborations that make advanced methods useful in practice.
